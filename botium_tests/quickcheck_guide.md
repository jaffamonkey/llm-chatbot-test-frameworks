# ✅ Quick Reference: NHS Compliance Chatbot Personas & Expected Behaviors

This is a **1-page checklist** for exploratory testing sessions. Use it to quickly recall personas and what to expect from the chatbot.

---

## 👤 Personas & Test Focus

### 1. Azim Patel – Healthcare Expert, Compliance Beginner
- Wants: **Plain English, step-by-step guidance**
- Test: Avoid jargon, explain acronyms (DSPT, GDPR).

### 2. Sarah Jenkins – Experienced Compliance Professional
- Wants: **Accurate, detailed answers**
- Test: Ensure no hallucinations, reference NHS docs.

### 3. Mark Thompson – Impatient / Annoyed User
- Wants: **Short, direct answers**
- Test: Bot stays calm, gives checklists + timeframes.

### 4. Lucy Wong – Neurodivergent User
- Wants: **Structured, repeatable steps**
- Test: Numbered lists, clear repetition on request.

### 5. Ahmed Khan – Small Business Owner, Time-Pressed
- Wants: **Time and cost estimates**
- Test: Practical advice, what can/can’t be outsourced.

### 6. Janet Morris – Skeptical User
- Wants: **Neutral, myth-busting responses**
- Test: Corrects misconceptions calmly, explains value.

---

## ⚡ Quick Tips for Testing
- Always check: **Clarity | Accuracy | Professionalism | Structure**
- Validate API responses: **<2s average**
- Ensure Bot handles: **ALL CAPS, blunt questions, repeat requests**
- Watch for: **Overly long answers, hallucinated compliance advice**
